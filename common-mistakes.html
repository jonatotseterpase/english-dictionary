📚 Common English Mistakes

Learn from hundreds of carefully prepared English lessons.

1. Subject–Verb Agreement

Mistake: He go to school every day.

Correct: He goes to school every day.

Rule: Singular subjects take singular verbs in the present tense.

2. Wrong Use of Tenses

Mistake: I have seen him yesterday.

Correct: I saw him yesterday.

Rule: Use the simple past tense with finished time expressions.

3. Confusing "Your" and "You're"

Mistake: Your welcome.

Correct: You're welcome.

Rule: "Your" shows possession, while "you're" means "you are".
