Lesson 1

Mistake

❌ I am having two brothers.

Correct

✅ I have two brothers.

Explanation

The verb have expresses possession. We normally do not use the continuous form (am having) when talking about ownership.

More Examples

  • ✅ I have a new car.
  • ✅ She has three sisters.
  • ❌ She is having three sisters.
